# KrankyBear Reader

This is a sample **Markdown** file, opened from *Help → Sample Files*.

## What it can render

- Headings, like the ones on this page
- **Bold**, *italic*, and `inline code`
- Bulleted and numbered lists
- Blockquotes
- Links, such as [the Fyne toolkit](https://fyne.io)
